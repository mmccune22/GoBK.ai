The Counseling Class

Before a bankruptcy case can be filed, you are required to take a short online class about money and credit. It’s easy — here’s what to know.

One short class

The class takes about an hour — online, on your phone or computer, at your own pace. We’ll give you a code to use when you sign up. When you finish, you get a certificate — that’s the document we need. The certificate is only good for 180 days, so it’s best to take the class soon. Watch the short video to see how it works.
